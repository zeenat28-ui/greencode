# GreenCode Compliance & Regulatory ESG Assurance

See full compliance and standards specifications in [docs/COMPLIANCE.md](docs/COMPLIANCE.md).

## Standards Conformance
- **GHG Protocol Corporate Standard**: Scope 2 operational electricity carbon calculation.
- **GHG Protocol Scope 3**: Category 1 & 2 embodied server hardware carbon accounting.
- **European CSRD (ESRS E1)**: Climate change, energy efficiency, and avoided emission reporting.
- **SEC Climate-Related Disclosures**: Financial and carbon impact materiality documentation.
- **Green Software Foundation (GSF)**: Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) v1.0 specification.
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Tamper-evident cryptographic SHA-256 hash-chained audit logging.
