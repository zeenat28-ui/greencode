## 3. Measurement Provenance & Certification Disclaimer
1. **Hardware vs. Model Provenance:** GreenCode reports `measurement_is_hardware: true` only when reading physical hardware counters (such as Linux RAPL `/sys/class/powercap`), and `measurement_is_hardware: false` (`measurement_method: "model"`) when returning TDP/SPECpower model estimates.
2. **Not an Certified Audit:** GreenCode implements the Green Software Foundation (GSF) SCI v1.0 formula (`SCI = (E * I + M) / R`) as an engineering calculation tool. Output generated by this software does **not** constitute an independently audited ISO 14064-1 or SOC 2 Type II certification.
