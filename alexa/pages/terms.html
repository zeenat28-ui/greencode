GreenCode Terms of Use

Effective 2026-10-07 · applies to the GreenCode Alexa+ add-on and MCP server.

The service

GreenCode audits source code for energy waste, computes Software Carbon Intensity per the Green Software Foundation SCI specification, and suggests refactors. It is provided as-is for informational purposes.

Not professional advice

Energy and carbon figures include their measurement method (hardware counter vs. TDP model) and data-source freshness. Modelled figures are estimates, not guarantees. Do not rely on GreenCode alone for regulatory reporting.

Your code

You are responsible for the code you submit. Do not submit material you have no right to process. GreenCode does not claim ownership of your code or of the results.

Safety

The refactor_code tool produces suggested edits. Apply them behind your own tests and review; GreenCode runs its behaviour-preservation gate, but it does not know your full system.
